The DILG DOSE Retooling Program 2025 is a capacity-building initiative of the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) designed to strengthen the competencies of DILG focal persons by equipping them with updated knowledge, skills, strategies and improve local government capacity development initiatives.
